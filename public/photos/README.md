Tips
- Resize to about 1600 px on the long side and keep each file under ~400 KB
  (squoosh.app is free and easy). Big phone photos (5–10 MB) make the site slow.
- Use lowercase names, `.jpg`, no spaces.
- Photo descriptions (Greek + English) are in `src/data/photos.ts`.
